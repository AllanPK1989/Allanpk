# PM — what to do  ·  பிஎம் — என்ன செய்வது

**Print this. Laminate it. Put one at every cell.**

---

## Every shift, before you start

**1. Open the PM list on the phone.**
**2. Pick YOUR NAME from the list. Every time.**

> Everyone signs in with the same account. Your name here is the **only** record of
> who did the work. Wrong name — someone else gets asked about your job.
>
> எல்லோரும் ஒரே கணக்கில் உள்நுழைகிறோம். உங்கள் பெயர் மட்டுமே யார்
> வேலை செய்தார் என்பதைக் காட்டும். எப்போதும் தேர்ந்தெடுக்கவும்.

---

## To do a PM  ·  பிஎம் செய்ய

| | Step | செய்ய வேண்டியது |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **SCAN** the sticker on the machine | ஸ்டிக்கரை ஸ்கேன் செய்யவும் |
| **2** | Check the **machine name** on screen is the machine in front of you | திரையில் உள்ள பெயரைச் சரிபார்க்கவும் |
| **3** | Tap **START PM** | START PM அழுத்தவும் |
| **4** | Work the checklist — **one check at a time** | ஒவ்வொன்றாகச் சரிபார்க்கவும் |
| **5** | Tap **SUBMIT** at the end | SUBMIT அழுத்தவும் |

**The machine leaves your list when you submit.** That is how you know it is done.

---

## On the checklist

**Read the ACCEPT line under each check.** It tells you the limit.

- **OK** — it meets the limit
- **NOT OK** — it does not. Then you **must**:
  - type what you saw
  - **take a photo**
- **A number box means type a number.** Not "ok". The actual reading.

> A bearing reading 48, then 52, then 57 across three PMs is a failure you can see
> coming — but only if you wrote the numbers down.

**Do not rush it.** The system records how long you took. A 45-minute checklist done
in 4 minutes gets flagged and someone will come and ask.

---

## The five buttons after you scan

| Button | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **START PM** | Beginning a planned PM |
| **COMPLETE CHECKLIST** | Doing the checks |
| **REPORT BREAKDOWN** | The machine has stopped or failed |
| **REQUEST SPARE** | You need a part |
| **LOG ABNORMALITY** | Something is not right yet, but has not failed — a leak, a noise, heat, vibration |

**Log abnormalities.** An air leak takes 20 seconds to record and costs money every
day it is not fixed. This is the most useful thing you can do in a spare minute.

---

## Reporting a breakdown

Fill in **both**:

- **Symptom** — what the operator saw
- **Root cause** — what was actually wrong

They are different. Keeping them separate is what lets us spot the same failure
coming back.

**Production loss minutes** = the **whole** time the machine could not run.
Include waiting for you, and waiting for a part. **Not just the repair time.**

---

## If something does not work

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Sticker will not scan | Wipe it. Still no — type the machine number in the app |
| Sticker damaged or missing | Tell your supervisor. **Do not guess a machine number** |
| No network | Carry on. The phone holds it and sends it when you are back in range |
| Wrong machine on screen | **Stop.** You scanned the wrong sticker. Go back and scan again |
| Machine not in your list | Scan it anyway — you can still report a breakdown or log an abnormality |

---

## Never

- **Never** pick someone else's name
- **Never** tick OK without looking
- **Never** guess a machine number
- **Never** leave a NOT OK with no photo and no note

---

## Why the counter matters

Every cell has a counter. It adds up the production hours.

**At 4,000 hours — or 6 months, whichever comes first — that cell needs a PM.**

The counter goes back to zero **only when EVERY machine in the cell is done.**
Three out of four is not done.

> When you finish the **last** machine in a cell, the app tells you. That was the one
> that closed the cell.
