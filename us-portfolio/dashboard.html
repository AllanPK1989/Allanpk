Concentration & risk

How the scores are built

Valuation score

  • 40% upside to consensus 12-month target.
  • 40% forward P/E against the sector median (30.4× semis, 19.9× software, 22× mega-cap).
  • 20% position inside the 52-week range — lower scores better.

Entry score

  • Peaks for a pullback of 12–35% off the 52-week high: deep enough to matter, not yet a broken chart.
  • Penalises names within 5% of the high (no margin of safety) and beyond 50% off it (falling knife).

What the score can't see

  • Cyclical peak earnings — a low P/E on top-of-cycle EPS reads as cheap and is not. MU is the live example.
  • Balance-sheet risk. CRWV scores well and carries $51.6bn of debt.
  • Consensus targets drift toward the price. Treat them as a crowd estimate, not a valuation.
