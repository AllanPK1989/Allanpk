    Tips for a natural sound
    • Quiet room, phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, same distance every time.
    • Tap record, wait half a second, then speak. Silence at the ends is trimmed automatically.
    • Read it as written (the on-screen captions show this exact text), but in your natural speaking style, like explaining to a friend.
    • Made a mistake? Tap record again on the same sentence to replace it.
    • You can stop any time. Your recordings stay on this phone until you save the zip.
