      Official sponsor lists

      A match means the employer holds a sponsor licence or has recently been approved to hire foreign workers. It does not guarantee that a particular job is open to sponsorship. Always confirm with the employer.

      Add another country's list (for example US H-1B employers)

      1. Download a CSV or XLSX of employers, for example from the USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub (choose a fiscal year, then export).
      2. Rename it so it starts with the country code, for example us-h1b-2025.csv.
      3. Put it in data/registers/import/ and press Update now.

      The file needs a column named like Employer, Petitioner, Organisation or Company.
